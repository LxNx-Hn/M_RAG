# Claim Policy

## Main Claim

The thesis claim is a factor analysis of HyDE, CAD, and Korean-target SCD on a
fixed Paper-RAG backbone. It is not a claim that the routed service layer is a
new retrieval method.

## Graduation-Project Layer

The A-F routed chatbot remains a service integration layer for paper review.
Its runtime routes are preserved for usability and qualitative system examples.
